# One Star, Five Stars for the Food

An alien tourist torches Earth in a one-star review — corrosive oxygen, rent charged for gravity, locals who eat with their faces — then ruins the whole rant by admitting it would cross three nebulae again for pizza, described in baffled outsider terms as a molten cheese disc. The whiplash between the grievance list and the dreamy food confession is the joke: the complaint reads like a warning, the subordinate clause reads like an advertisement.
